Sight Reading Trainer Account Deletion

You can request permanent deletion of your Sight Reading Trainer account and associated cloud data.

Delete from within the app

Open Sight Reading Trainer → Menu → Account → Delete Account and confirm the deletion request.

Request deletion without the app

If you cannot access the app, send an account-deletion request to the support email listed on the Sight Reading Trainer Google Play store listing.

Use the subject Sight Reading Trainer Account Deletion and include the Google account email used to sign in to Sight Reading Trainer so the account can be identified. Additional verification may be requested before deletion.

What is deleted

Deletion removes the Sight Reading Trainer cloud account and associated cloud records, including synchronized gem economy data, gem transaction records, reward progress/state, synchronization metadata, and cloud instrument entitlements associated with that account.

What is not automatically deleted

Data stored only on your device is not automatically erased by cloud account deletion. This can include local decks, local practice history, settings, downloaded instrument audio, and local economy/entitlement state. You can remove local app data using device/app controls.

Processing time and retention

Deletion requests will be processed after the account is identified and any required verification is completed. Certain limited records may be retained when required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or transaction obligations.
