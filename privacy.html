Sight Reading Trainer Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sight Reading Trainer handles information when you use the app.

Information processed on your device

Sight Reading Trainer stores app settings, decks, practice history, downloaded instrument audio, and other local app data on your device. Rhythm microphone input is used to detect musical timing. Microphone audio is processed on-device for rhythm analysis and is not intentionally uploaded or stored as an audio recording by Sight Reading Trainer.

Optional account information

If you choose to sign in with Google, the app may receive account information such as your name, email address, Google/Firebase user identifier, and profile information made available through the sign-in service. This information is used for account management and to synchronize supported account data.

Cloud-synchronized app data

For signed-in users, Sight Reading Trainer may store and synchronize gem economy information and instrument entitlements using Google Firebase services. This can include gem balances and transactions, practice-reward progress, daily reward state, synchronization metadata, and unlocked instruments. Decks, ordinary practice history, downloaded instrument audio, and general app settings are not intended to be included in this account sync unless the app is changed in the future and this policy is updated.

Advertising

The app may offer voluntary rewarded advertisements through Google AdMob. Ads are not shown elsewhere in the app. Google and its advertising services may process device identifiers, advertising data, approximate location inferred from network information, app interactions, diagnostics, and other data as described by Google's policies. Sight Reading Trainer awards gems only after a rewarded-ad completion is confirmed.

Purchases

Gem purchases are processed through Google Play on Android. Sight Reading Trainer may receive and store transaction identifiers, product identifiers, purchase status, and related entitlement information needed to credit and reconcile purchases. The app does not receive your full payment-card details from Google Play.

How information is used

Information is used to provide app functionality, authenticate optional accounts, synchronize gems and instrument entitlements, process and reconcile purchases, provide voluntary rewarded ads, prevent duplicate transactions, maintain security, and troubleshoot service failures.

Service providers

Sight Reading Trainer uses services provided by Google, including Firebase/Google Sign-In, Cloud Firestore, Google AdMob, and Google Play Billing. Those providers may process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Data security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect synchronized information, including authenticated access controls and encrypted network connections provided by the applicable platform services. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Retention and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until removed through app controls, app data deletion, or uninstalling, subject to platform behavior. Cloud account information is retained while your Sight Reading Trainer account exists and as needed to operate the service. You can request deletion of your account and associated cloud data from within the app or through the Account Deletion page. When a cloud account is deleted, associated Sight Reading Trainer cloud account records are deleted, subject to any limited retention required for security, fraud prevention, legal, or transaction-record obligations. As designed, deleting the cloud account does not automatically erase data stored locally on your device.

Children

Sight Reading Trainer is a music-education application. Account, advertising, and purchase features must be configured and operated in accordance with applicable age, family, advertising, and platform requirements. This policy will be updated if the app's target-audience configuration or child-directed treatment changes.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the app, service providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions or account-deletion requests, contact the developer using the support contact listed on the Sight Reading Trainer Google Play listing.
